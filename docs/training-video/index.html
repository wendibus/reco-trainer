Privacy note

The video uses synthetic demonstration footage only. Reco Trainer processes training videos, frames, labels, models, and benchmark results locally. The illustrative model check is not a substitute for evaluation on a larger, representative, previously unseen dataset.

Reco Trainer is ready for real-world use but not yet bug-free. Validate labels, benchmark results, and exported models before relying on them.
