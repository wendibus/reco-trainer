# Reco Trainer 0.31.1 — Guide for the Augmentation Studio

Videos, frames, labels, checkpoints, and metrics remain entirely on the local computer.

## New

- **Step-by-step guide in the Augmentation Studio.** When the studio opens, a guide with seven illustrated steps explains exactly what to do: what you need first (a prepared working folder, **Set up ML**, at least 300, ideally 500 reviewed images from several recordings), choosing the source folder and the role per class, the settings (1,500 to 2,000 variations for a first test), checking venues and teams, generating, and comparing **Real + synthetic** with **Real only, same split** in **Test models**.
- **Don't show again** hides it. The **Guide** button in the studio opens it again, and unticking the box there shows it at every start once more.
- Available in German, English, Spanish and French; the screenshots show the studio in the same language. They are drawn sample pictures, not real footage.
